GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for learners visiting Wealth Research Desk.

Are the learning resources free?

This website is designed to share free introductory learning resources. Any paid service, if introduced later, should be clearly identified.

Is this an official NISM website?

No. Wealth Research Desk is an independent educational resource and is not presented as an official NISM website. Refer to NISM for official exam information.

Can I prepare in Hindi and English?

Yes. Use the language toggle in the navigation for supported bilingual text. Some course descriptions and video content may be available in one language until translated.

Does completing a quiz guarantee passing an exam?

No. Sample quizzes are for practice only. Exam success depends on your preparation and the official exam requirements.
